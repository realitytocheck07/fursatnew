# Fursat website: how to change "What's new"

You can change the **What's new** part of the Home page yourself. It takes 2 minutes. You do not need a developer.

**Open:** <https://fursat.sanity.studio> and log in with the email we invited. Click **What's new**.
[screenshot: Studio login and the What's new list, with the Active now / Scheduled / Ended labels]

Use Studio only. You never need the "Manage" or "API" pages. Please do not open them.

**Nothing goes on the website until you press Publish.** Until then, your card is a draft and only you can see it.

---

## 1. Add an offer
1. Click the **+** (create) button to make a new card.
2. **Type:** Offer.
3. Fill in **Title**, **Short text**, and **Offer terms** (one line).
4. Add a **Photo** if you have one (see photo rules below).
5. Set **Start date** and **End date**. An offer must have an End date.
6. Check the preview on the right. Then press **Publish**.

[screenshot: new card form with the live preview and the character counters]

## 2. Add a festival wish
1. Click the **+** (create) button to make a new card. **Type:** Wish.
2. Write the **Title**, for example "Happy Diwali from all of us at Fursat". Add **Short text**.
3. Set **Start date** and **End date** (required). No photo is needed.
4. Press **Publish**.

A wish shows as a **gold banner**, not as a card.

## 3. Pin a card
Open the card. Switch on **Pin to top**. Press **Publish**.
Only **1 card** can be pinned. To pin another, open the old pinned card and switch **Pin to top** off first.

## 4. End a card early
Open the card and press **Unpublish**. It leaves the website in 1 to 3 minutes. The card stays in your list as a draft, so you can use it again later.

## 5. Remove a card
Open the card, click the **...** menu, press **Delete**. This cannot be undone. If you may want the card again, use **Unpublish** instead.

[screenshot: the card menu showing Unpublish and Delete]

## What each field means

| Field | What to write | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Offer, Event, News or Wish | pick one |
| **Title** | The big words on the card | **60 characters** |
| **Short text** | One or two simple lines | **160 characters** |
| **Photo** | A real Fursat photo | optional for News and Event |
| **Alt text** | One short sentence about the photo, for people who cannot see it | needed with every photo |
| **Real-photo tick** | You promise: "This is a real photo of Fursat or our food, and we may use it" | needed with every photo |
| **Button label** | The words on the button, for example "Order on WhatsApp" | **24 characters** |
| **Button goes to** | A page on our site (pick from the list), WhatsApp with a message already written, or a link | pick one |
| **Start date / End date** | When the card shows. End must come after Start | Offers and Wishes **must** have an End date |
| **Offer terms** | One line of conditions. A "Terms apply" link is added for you | needed for Offers |
| **Pin to top** | Shows this card first and bigger | only 1 at a time |
| **Order** | A number. Small numbers come first | optional |

Studio counts your characters and shows a message if something is missing. Fix it, then Publish.

Only post offers that the management has confirmed. Do not guess a price or a date.

[screenshot: Start and End date fields, and the Offer terms line]

## Photo rules
- **Real photos of Fursat only:** our food, our drinks, our place.
- **No stock photos. No AI pictures.** Not from Google, not from other restaurants.
- **No faces without consent.** If you can see a guest's or a team member's face, you need their OK first. If you are not sure, choose another photo.
- **At least 1200 pixels wide.** Use the original photo from the phone or camera. Photos sent through WhatsApp or downloaded from Instagram are often too small.
- Click the **crop point** on the most important part (for example the dish), so phones do not cut it off.
- Write the alt text and switch on the real-photo tick.

[screenshot: photo upload with the crop point, alt text and real-photo tick]

## How long until it is on the website?
- After you press **Publish** or **Unpublish**: about **1 to 3 minutes**. Refresh the Home page.
- A card with a **Start date in the future** shows when that day comes. The site updates itself every morning at about **6:00 AM**.
- When the **End date** passes, the card goes away by itself.
- Not showing after 5 minutes? Check that you pressed **Publish** (not just Save), that the dates are right, and refresh. Still nothing? Call us.

## "What's new settings"
One settings page on top of the list. You can turn the section on or off, change the small heading and the big heading, and choose how many cards show (up to **6**).
